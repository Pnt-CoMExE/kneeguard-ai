Part 3 · Proposed decision model

Decision model design

Multilabel classification with optional multimodal training. Outputs become triage decisions under human oversight. Algorithm details marked PLACEHOLDER await teammate research lock-in.

Selected model type

Primary: Multilabel classification model (imaging backbone + study-level aggregation).
Optional hybrid: Report NLP / LLM for weak label extraction during training only.

Why this over alternatives: the competition target is simultaneous detection of twelve findings, evaluated with macro-averaged AUC-ROC. Pure clustering does not produce calibrated finding probabilities; a single regression target cannot express multilabel clinical structure.

PLACEHOLDER Final backbone (e.g., 2.5D CNN / transformer / ensemble) and baseline comparison table from teammate experiments.

  • Baseline: per-finding mean prevalence predictor

Model workflow

Ten-step flow from the project guidance, adapted to this case.

1

Data collection

Obtain competition MRI studies, metadata, and training reports under license rules.

2

Data validation

Check study IDs, series presence, label schema, and report availability.

3

Cleaning & preprocessing

Normalize intensities, select informative series, handle missing labels.

4

Feature engineering

Slice sampling, embeddings, optional report-derived weak labels.

5

Model training

Train multilabel model with group-aware validation folds.

6

Validation & testing

Macro AUC-ROC, per-finding curves, runtime checks for efficiency constraints.

7

Result interpretation

Calibrate scores; inspect failure cases with clinician partners.

8

Decision recommendation

Map probabilities to Low / Medium / High triage bands.

9

User action

Radiologist reviews prioritized findings and records the final report.

10

Feedback & improvement

Log disagreements; retrain or recalibrate on approved cohorts.

Evaluation & success criteria

  • Primary competition metric: macro-averaged AUC-ROC across findings
  • Secondary: per-finding AUC, precision/recall at operating points
  • Efficiency track: predictive performance vs runtime (if pursued)
  • Decision-support usefulness rated by clinician reviewers
Draft success criterion: The model is considered successful if macro AUC-ROC ≥ TBD and at least 80% of reviewed triage recommendations are judged useful by test users.

Decision rules

Example mapping from max high-priority finding probability.

Model output Level Recommended action
All priority findings < 0.40 Low Routine queue; standard reporting workflow
Any priority finding 0.40–0.69 Medium Prioritize review; notify responsible radiologist
Any critical finding ≥ 0.70 High Immediate specialist review; document override

Thresholds are placeholders and require clinical agreement. Human approval is always required before patient-facing action.

Benefits, limitations, ethics & risks

Expected benefits

Faster triage, more consistent screening across sites, support where MSK specialists are scarce, and structured second-read assistance for complex multilabel exams.

Limitations

Domain shift across scanners, incomplete labels, class imbalance, and possible over-reliance on report-derived weak supervision that may encode reporting style bias.

Ethics & risks

False negatives on fracture/ligament injury, fairness across sites/languages, privacy of imaging data, limited explainability, and the need for continuous human oversight.

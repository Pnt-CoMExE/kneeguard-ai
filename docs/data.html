Dataset relevance

The dataset is suitable because it directly encodes the decision task: estimate clinically important knee findings from real multi-site MRI exams. Training-time reports support weak labeling and multimodal learning; inference-time reports are not provided, matching a realistic deployment constraint.

Diversity across scanners, protocols, languages, and sites strengthens external validity, but also introduces domain shift. Prevalence of abnormalities may differ across train / public / private splits.

Known limitations

  • Only a subset of training studies may carry full per-condition labels.
  • Intensities, orientations, and resolutions vary across series.
  • Transfer syntaxes and metadata are constrained / stripped.
  • Class imbalance is expected across rare findings.
  • Report language diversity complicates text pipelines.
